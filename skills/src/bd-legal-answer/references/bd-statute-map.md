# Bangladesh Statute Map — situation → likely law

Use this to recognise which statute is probably relevant to the user's situation. It is a
routing aid only — always cite the actual provision from the retrieved context, not from
this map. Section numbers here are common anchors, not a substitute for the corpus.

## Criminal — general
- **Penal Code 1860** — the core criminal code: theft (§378–379), cheating (§415–420),
  criminal breach of trust (§405), assault & criminal force (§350–358), hurt/grievous hurt
  (§319–326), criminal intimidation/threats (§503–506), defamation (§499–500), wrongful
  confinement (§339–342), criminal trespass (§441).
- **Code of Criminal Procedure (CrPC) 1898** — *procedure*, not offences: how an FIR is
  lodged (§154), police investigation, arrest, cognizable vs non-cognizable offences,
  bail, the powers of magistrates. Use this for "how do I file a case / report to police".

## Women & children
- **Nari-o-Shishu Nirjatan Daman Ain 2000** (Prevention of Oppression Against Women and
  Children Act) — rape, acid violence, trafficking, sexual harassment, grievous harm,
  dowry death. Tried in special tribunals. The primary statute for serious gender violence.
- **Domestic Violence (Prevention and Protection) Act 2010** — physical, psychological,
  sexual, and economic abuse within the family/household; protection orders, residence
  orders. Use for "my husband/in-laws beat me / threw me out / control my money".
- **Dowry Prohibition Act 1980** — giving, taking, or demanding dowry.
- **Child Marriage Restraint Act 2017** — marriage below legal age (21 male / 18 female).

## Digital & online
- **Cyber Security Act 2023** (replaced the Digital Security Act 2018) — online harassment,
  blackmail with images, identity theft / fake profiles, hacking, publishing defamatory or
  obscene material online, non-consensual intimate images. Use for any "online / Facebook /
  WhatsApp / leaked photos / fake ID" harm.

## Family law (personal-law dependent)
- Muslim Family Laws Ordinance 1961 — marriage registration, polygamy, talaq procedure,
  maintenance, inheritance shares (Muslim).
- Family Courts Ordinance 1985 — jurisdiction for dower, maintenance, custody, guardianship.
- Personal law varies by religion (Muslim / Hindu / Christian) — flag this and recommend a
  lawyer for inheritance and marriage matters.

## Property & land
- Registration Act 1908, State Acquisition and Tenancy Act 1950, Transfer of Property Act
  1882 — deeds, mutation (নামজারি), record of rights (খতিয়ান), possession disputes.
  Land disputes are fact-heavy — almost always route to a lawyer and the local Land Office.
  (For *reading* a specific land document, hand off to `bd-legal-doc-reader`.)

## Labour & employment
- **Bangladesh Labour Act 2006** — wages, unpaid dues, wrongful termination, working hours,
  maternity benefit, workplace safety, gratuity.

## Consumer & money
- Consumer Rights Protection Act 2009 — defective goods, overcharging, fraud by sellers.
- Negotiable Instruments Act 1881 §138 — dishonoured (bounced) cheques.

## Constitutional baseline
- **Constitution of Bangladesh** — fundamental rights (Part III): equality (Art 27),
  non-discrimination (Art 28), protection of law (Art 31), life & liberty (Art 32),
  safeguards on arrest (Art 33). Cite when the question is about a basic right or due process.
