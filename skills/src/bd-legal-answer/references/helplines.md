# Bangladesh helplines & referral numbers

Surface these when the situation calls for it. Keep them current with
`services/rag/app/pipeline/stage6_output.py` (the safety/exit-ramp templates).

## Emergency (immediate physical danger)
- **National Emergency / Police: 999**
- **Women & Child Affairs helpline: 109**
- **Child Helpline: 1098**
- Fire Service & Civil Defence: 199

In an emergency, stop answering as a legal query — tell the user to use the Anchor Alert
feature (press-and-hold the red button on the Home screen) and call 999.

## Crisis / emotional support (self-harm signals)
- **Kaan Pete Roi** (emotional support, 24/7): 01779-554391
- **National Mental Health Helpline**: 16789

Do not provide crisis counselling yourself — route to the numbers above and to a trusted
person.

## Free legal aid
- **Bangladesh Legal Aid Services Trust (BLAST)**: 02-9880064
- National Legal Aid Services Organisation (NLASO) helpline: 16430
- Ain o Salish Kendra (ASK) — legal aid and human rights support.

## When to refer to a lawyer (not just info)
- Any court appearance, summons, or warrant.
- Land transactions and possession disputes.
- Signing a contract or deed.
- Marriage, divorce, custody, inheritance.
- Anything where a deadline is approaching or money/liberty is at stake.

Anchor has a verified lawyer directory — offer to connect the user to a lawyer who
specialises in the relevant area.
