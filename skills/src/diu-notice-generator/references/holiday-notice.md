# Holiday / Closure Notices

Covers public holidays, semester breaks, and unscheduled closures (weather, strikes, etc.).

## Must include
- Reason for closure (holiday name or cause)
- Exact closure dates
- When normal operations resume
- Whether online classes / offices continue, if applicable

## Tone
Formal and informative. For unscheduled closures, add a brief reassurance and a pointer
to where updates will be posted.

## Example (English)
```
Dear All,

In observance of Eid-ul-Adha, the university will remain closed from 6 to 10 June 2026.
Academic and administrative activities will resume on 11 June 2026.

For any urgent matters during the holiday, please contact the relevant office by email.

Registrar's Office
Daffodil International University
```
