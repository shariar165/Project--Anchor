# Library Notices

Covers reading-hall hours, access rules, room reservations, and overdue dues.

## Must include
- What is changing (hours, access, service)
- Effective dates and times
- Any condition (ID after a certain hour, advance reservation, fines)

## Tone
Helpful and clear. Library notices are routine; keep them brief.

## Example (English)
```
Dear Students,

During the final examinations of Spring 2026 (8–15 June), the central library reading hall
will remain open until 1:00 AM. Photo ID is required for entry after 10:00 PM, and group
study rooms must be reserved in advance through the library portal.

We wish you a productive finals week.

Registrar's Office
Daffodil International University
```
