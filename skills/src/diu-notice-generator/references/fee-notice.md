# Fee / Payment Notices

Covers tuition instalments, late fees, payment deadlines, and payment channels.

## Must include
- What payment is due (instalment, registration fee, etc.)
- Deadline date (and late-fee terms if applicable)
- Accepted payment channels (bank, portal, mobile financial services)
- Consequence of non-payment (e.g. blocked registration), stated factually

## Tone
Formal and unambiguous — money matters must be exact. Avoid alarming language.

## Example (English)
```
Dear Students,

The second instalment of tuition fees for Spring 2026 must be paid by 20 June 2026.
Payments can be made through the student portal or at any branch of the designated bank
using your student ID.

Students who fail to pay by the deadline will be charged a late fee and may be unable to
register for the next semester. Kindly settle your dues on time.

Registrar's Office
Daffodil International University
```
