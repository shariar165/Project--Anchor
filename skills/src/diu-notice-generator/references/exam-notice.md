# Examination Notices

Covers exam routines, hall/seat plans, results publication, and exam permissions.

## Must include
- Exam name and term (e.g. "Final Examinations, Spring 2026")
- Exact dates and times
- Venue / hall, or where the seat plan will be published
- Any entry requirement (photo ID, admit card, clearance of dues)
- Reporting time before the exam

## Tone
Strict and precise — students rely on these for scheduling. State consequences of
non-compliance factually (e.g. "Candidates without an admit card will not be permitted
to sit for the examination.").

## Example (English)
```
Dear Students,

The Final Examinations of Spring 2026 will be held from 8 to 15 June 2026. The detailed
seat plan will be published on the student portal by 5 June 2026.

Candidates must bring their admit card and university photo ID, and report to the
examination hall at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start time. Clearance of all
outstanding dues is required before collecting the admit card.

Registrar's Office
Daffodil International University
```
