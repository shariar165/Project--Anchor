# Hostel / Residential Notices

Covers hall rules, maintenance, curfew changes, room allocation, and dues for residents.

## Must include
- Which hall(s) the notice applies to
- What is changing and when
- Any action residents must take (vacate, register, pay, comply)

## Tone
Formal and direct. Hall notices often carry compliance requirements — state them clearly
without being harsh.

## Example (English)
```
Dear Residents,

Maintenance work on the water supply system of Female Hall 2 will take place on
12 June 2026 from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Water service may be interrupted during this period.

Residents are requested to store water in advance and cooperate with the maintenance team.

Registrar's Office
Daffodil International University
```
