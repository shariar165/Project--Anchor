# Event / Seminar Notices

Covers seminars, workshops, convocations, club events, and guest lectures.

## Must include
- Event name and purpose
- Date, time, and venue
- Who should attend / how to register
- Any requirement (registration link, dress code, ID)

## Tone
Formal but inviting. It is acceptable to be slightly warmer than an exam notice while
remaining institutional.

## Example (English)
```
Dear Students,

The Department of Software Engineering will host a seminar on "Careers in AI" on
18 June 2026 at 3:00 PM in Auditorium B. The session features industry speakers and a
Q&A panel.

Seats are limited. Please register through the student portal by 16 June 2026.

Registrar's Office
Daffodil International University
```
