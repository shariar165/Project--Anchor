# Attendance Shortage / Leave Application

## Purpose
Request condoning of attendance shortage OR apply for leave of absence from classes
(medical, personal, or official).

## Recipient
Head of Department / Class Teacher / Dean / Examination Committee

## Sub-types:

### A. Attendance Shortage Condonation
- Current attendance % vs required %
- Period of absence and reason
- Evidence: medical certificate, travel proof, etc.
- Assurance of future regular attendance

### B. Medical Leave
- Date range of illness
- Doctor/hospital name
- Medical certificate attached

### C. Official / University-Related Leave
- What the official engagement was (conference, sports, NCC, etc.)
- Date range
- Proof from the organizing body

## Standard Subject Line
`Application for Condonation of Attendance Shortage — [Name] | [ID] | [Course Code]`

## Key Phrases
- "My attendance in [Course Name] has fallen to [X]% due to [reason]..."
- "I was absent from [date] to [date] due to [illness/emergency/official duty]..."
- "I am attaching a medical certificate issued by Dr. [Name] of [Hospital]..."
- "I assure you that I will maintain regular attendance henceforth..."
- "I kindly request that my shortage be condoned on humanitarian/medical grounds..."
