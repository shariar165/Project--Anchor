# Late Fee Payment Application

## Purpose
Request permission to submit tuition or semester fee after the official deadline has passed.

## Recipient
Typically: The Treasurer / Finance Officer / Registrar / Dean of Student Affairs

## Key Information to Collect
- Original fee deadline
- Current date (how many days late)
- Fee amount (if known)
- Reason for delay (financial hardship, banking issue, family emergency, etc.)
- Any supporting documents (bank statement, medical certificate, family letter)
- Whether a fine/penalty is being requested for waiver or just permission to pay late

## Tone Notes
This is a sensitive application — many students face genuine financial hardship.
Write with dignity: acknowledge the delay without being overly self-deprecating.

## Standard Subject Line
`Application for Permission to Submit Late Fee — [Name] | [ID] | [Program]`

## Key Phrases
- "Due to unavoidable financial circumstances..."
- "I was unable to arrange the required funds within the stipulated deadline..."
- "I assure you that the fee will be submitted by [specific date]..."
- "I kindly request that the late fine be waived / that I be allowed to submit the fee without penalty..."

## Sample Reason Paragraphs

**Financial hardship:**
> "Due to a sudden financial constraint in my family, I was unable to arrange the required fee amount within the stipulated deadline. My father/guardian faced [brief situation], which delayed the availability of funds. I have now arranged the fee and am in a position to submit it immediately upon your approval."

**Banking/technical issue:**
> "I attempted to submit my semester fee on [date] through [bank/portal], however, the transaction was unsuccessful due to a technical error. I have attached the transaction record as evidence. I am requesting permission to resubmit the fee without penalty."

## Documents to Mention (if available)
- Bank transaction failure screenshot
- Family income certificate or affidavit
- Medical certificate (if illness caused the delay)
