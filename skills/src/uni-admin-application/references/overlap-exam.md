# Overlap / Clash Exam Application

## Purpose
Request rescheduling or special arrangement for an examination when two or more exams
are scheduled at the same date and time (overlap/clash).

## Recipient
Controller of Examinations / Examination Committee / Head of Department

## Key Information to Collect
- Course 1: name, code, scheduled date and time
- Course 2 (and more if applicable): name, code, scheduled date and time
- Proof of clash (mention the exam schedule)
- Requested solution: reschedule one exam OR sit both on different days OR special paper

## Tone Notes
This is an administrative request — keep it factual and solution-focused.
Attach or reference the official exam timetable as evidence.

## Standard Subject Line
`Application for Resolution of Examination Schedule Clash — [Name] | [ID]`

## Structure Notes

**Paragraph 2 should clearly state:**
- Exact courses clashing
- Exact date and time of the clash
- That this is beyond the student's control (they did not schedule the exams)

**Paragraph 3 — Proposed Solution:**
Suggest a specific resolution. Don't leave it open-ended:
- "I kindly request that my [Course X] exam be rescheduled to [alternative date/time]"
- "I request permission to sit [Course X] as a special/makeup exam"

## Key Phrases
- "My examination schedule contains a direct clash between..."
- "Both papers are scheduled on [date] at [time], making it impossible for me to appear in both..."
- "I humbly request that special arrangements be made..."
- "I am enclosing a copy of the official examination timetable for your reference..."

## Common Variations
- **Three-way clash**: List all three courses; request rescheduling of whichever is easiest
- **Clash due to makeup exam**: If one exam is a makeup from a previous absence, note that specifically
