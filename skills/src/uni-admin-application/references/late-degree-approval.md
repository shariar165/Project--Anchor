# Late Degree Approval Application

## Purpose
Request approval to receive, collect, or process a degree certificate after the standard
timeline has passed, OR request that a pending degree be processed/expedited.

## Recipient
Controller of Examinations / Registrar / Degree Section / Academic Office

## Common Scenarios

### A. Degree Collection After Deadline
Student did not collect degree during the official ceremony or collection period.
- Original collection date/ceremony
- Reason for missing it (abroad, illness, financial, etc.)
- Requesting alternative collection or postal delivery

### B. Degree Processing Delay (Student's Side)
Student submitted documents late for degree processing.
- What was submitted late (clearance form, dues, photos, etc.)
- Reason for the delay
- Confirmation that everything is now complete

### C. Urgent Degree Request
Student needs degree urgently (job offer, visa application, higher studies admission).
- Urgency reason
- Deadline they are facing
- Request for expedited processing

### D. Degree Correction
Student's degree contains an error (name, program, year).
- What the error is
- Correct information
- Supporting documents (CNIC, admission letter, marksheet)

## Standard Subject Line
`Application for Late Degree Approval / Collection — [Name] | [ID] | [Program, Year of Graduation]`

## Key Phrases
- "I request your kind approval for the collection/processing of my degree at this late stage..."
- "I was unable to collect my degree during the official period due to..."
- "I urgently require my degree for [job/visa/admission] purposes, with a deadline of [date]..."
- "I have completed all dues clearances and submitted the required documents..."
- "I would be grateful if the degree could be dispatched to the address provided..."
