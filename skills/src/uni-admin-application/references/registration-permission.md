# Registration Permission Application

## Purpose
Request permission to register for courses after the official registration deadline,
or to register for a specific course that requires special approval (overload, prerequisite
waiver, cross-department enrollment, etc.).

## Recipient
Head of Department / Registrar / Academic Advisor / Dean

## Sub-types:

### A. Late Registration
Missed the registration window.
- Original deadline
- Reason for missing it
- Which semester/courses to register for

### B. Course Overload Permission
Requesting more credit hours than the standard limit.
- Current CGPA (strong CGPA supports overload requests)
- How many extra credit hours requested
- Reason (final semester, want to graduate on time, etc.)

### C. Prerequisite Waiver
Requesting to take a course without completing its prerequisite.
- Course name and code
- Missing prerequisite
- Reason: equivalent knowledge, transferred credit, etc.

### D. Cross-Department / Elective Registration
Requesting to register in another department's course.
- Course name, code, offering department
- Why this course (relevance to degree, elective requirement)

## Standard Subject Line
`Application for [Late / Course / Overload] Registration Permission — [Name] | [ID]`

## Key Phrases
- "I request your kind permission to register for the following course(s) in [Semester, Year]..."
- "I was unable to complete registration within the deadline due to..."
- "My CGPA of [X.XX] reflects my academic capability to handle the additional workload..."
- "I assure you that I will meet all academic requirements for the registered courses..."

## Formatting Tip
When listing courses, use a simple inline list:
"I wish to register for: (1) [Course Name] [Code], (2) [Course Name] [Code]"
