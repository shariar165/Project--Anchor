# Mid-Term / Final Examination Permission Application

## Purpose
Request permission to appear in an exam when the student has been barred or is at risk of
being barred due to attendance shortage, fee default, form submission failure, or other
administrative reason.

## Recipient
Controller of Examinations / Head of Department / Dean / Examination Committee

## Sub-types — ask which applies:

### A. Attendance Shortage
Student lacks the minimum attendance % required to sit the exam.
- Minimum attendance threshold (e.g., 75%) — mention if known
- Actual attendance % the student has
- Reason for absences (illness, family emergency, travel, etc.)
- Evidence: medical certificate, travel documents, etc.

### B. Fee Default / Form Not Submitted
Student did not submit exam form or fee in time.
- Original deadline
- Reason for missing it
- Current readiness to pay/submit immediately

### C. General Permission (no specific bar)
Student is requesting advance permission or confirmation to appear.
- State the exam name/date
- Mention any concern (e.g., result not yet uploaded, ID issue, etc.)

## Standard Subject Line
`Application for Permission to Appear in [Mid-Term / Final] Examination — [Name] | [ID]`

## Key Phrases
- "I humbly request that I be allowed to appear in the forthcoming examination..."
- "My absence was due to [reason], which was beyond my control..."
- "I am enclosing a [medical certificate / supporting document] for your kind consideration..."
- "I assure you that such a situation will not arise in future..."
- "My attendance shortage is due to [specific cause] and not due to academic negligence..."

## Sample Attendance Paragraph
> "I regret to inform you that my attendance in [Course Name, Course Code] has fallen short of
> the required 75% due to [reason, e.g., prolonged illness during the months of October–November].
> I have attached a medical certificate issued by [doctor/hospital] as supporting evidence.
> Despite my absence, I have maintained my coursework and am fully prepared for the examination."

## Important Note
Always end with a commitment: "I assure you that I will ensure full attendance in the future."
This signals to the committee that the student takes the matter seriously.
