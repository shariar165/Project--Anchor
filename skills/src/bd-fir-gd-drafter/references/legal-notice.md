# Legal Notice (লিগ্যাল নোটিশ)

A legal notice is a formal written demand sent to a person/organisation **before** starting
civil litigation. It states the facts, makes a specific demand, sets a deadline for
compliance, and warns of legal consequences if ignored. It is commonly the required first
step in money-recovery, breach-of-contract, defamation, and tenancy disputes, and is often
sent through an advocate.

## When a legal notice
- Money owed / loan not repaid / dues unpaid.
- Breach of contract or agreement.
- Defamation, or to demand a retraction/apology.
- Cheque dishonour (Negotiable Instruments Act 1881 §138 — notice is mandatory before suit).
- Eviction / tenancy demands.

## Structure

```
LEGAL NOTICE

[Optional: Through — Advocate [Name], [Chamber Address]]

Date: [DD Month YYYY]

To,
[Recipient Full Name]
[Recipient Address]

Subject: Legal notice for [e.g. recovery of Tk ___ / breach of agreement dated ___]

Sir/Madam,

Under instructions from and on behalf of my client, [Client Name], of [Address], I serve
upon you this legal notice as follows:

1. [Fact 1 — the relationship/agreement and relevant dates.]
2. [Fact 2 — what you did or failed to do, with dates and amounts.]
3. [Fact 3 — the resulting loss/harm to my client.]

You are therefore called upon to [the specific demand — e.g. pay Tk ___ / perform ___ /
publish a written apology] within [7 / 15 / 30] days of receipt of this notice.

Take notice that if you fail to comply within the said period, my client will be
constrained to initiate appropriate legal proceedings against you, civil and/or criminal,
entirely at your risk as to cost and consequences.

[Signature]
[Sender / Advocate Name]
[Address · Phone]
```

## Notes
- State a clear, reasonable compliance period (commonly 7–30 days, per the matter).
- Keep the demand specific and quantified — vague demands are hard to enforce.
- Send by a traceable method (registered post / courier with acknowledgement) and keep proof.
- For §138 cheque-bounce notices there are strict statutory time limits — recommend a lawyer.
