# First Information Report (FIR / এজাহার)

An FIR is the first report of a **cognizable offence** to the police, lodged under §154 of
the Code of Criminal Procedure 1898. It is what sets a criminal investigation in motion.
For serious offences against women and children, the case is tried in a special tribunal
under the Nari-o-Shishu Nirjatan Daman Ain 2000.

## When an FIR (not a GD)
Cognizable offences — the police can investigate and arrest without a magistrate's prior
order: theft/robbery, assault and grievous hurt, dowry demand, rape and sexual violence,
acid violence, abduction, cyber blackmail, criminal intimidation, etc.

## Structure

```
To
The Officer-in-Charge
[Name] Police Station (থানা), [District]

Subject: First Information Report regarding [nature of offence]

Sir,

I, [Full Name], [age], son/daughter of [Father's/Mother's Name], residing at [Full
Address], NID No. [number], phone [number], lodge this report and state as follows:

1. Date, time and place of occurrence: [be specific].

2. Accused: [Name(s) and address(es) if known, and relationship to me; otherwise
   "unknown/unidentified person(s)"].

3. Account of the incident: [Clear chronological narration — what happened, how it began,
   what each person did, what was said. Stick to facts you witnessed or can support.]

4. Injuries / loss / damage: [injuries sustained, items/amount stolen with identifiers,
   medical treatment taken].

5. Witnesses: [names and addresses, if any].

6. Evidence: [documents, messages, photos, CCTV, medical report — what you can produce].

The above offence appears to attract [likely applicable sections — e.g. Penal Code 1860
§ ___ / Nari-o-Shishu Nirjatan Daman Ain 2000 § ___ — to be confirmed by the authorities].

I therefore pray that an FIR be registered and necessary legal action be taken.

Yours faithfully,
[Signature]
[Full Name]
[Address] · [Phone]
Date: [DD Month YYYY]
```

## Notes
- The police, not the complainant, finalise the sections charged — suggest, don't assert.
- If the police refuse to register an FIR, the complainant may approach the Superintendent
  of Police or file a petition before the Magistrate (§156(3) CrPC) — mention this only if
  relevant, and recommend a lawyer.
- Be precise about date/time/place — vagueness weakens the report.
