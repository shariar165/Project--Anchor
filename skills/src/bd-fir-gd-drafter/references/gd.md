# General Diary (GD / জিডি)

A GD is an entry recorded at a police station for matters that are **not** cognizable
offences, or to put something on record: a lost document/phone, a threat, harassment that
hasn't yet escalated, a missing person (initial), or a preventive complaint. It does not by
itself start a criminal prosecution, but it creates an official, dated record.

## When a GD (not an FIR)
- Lost NID, passport, certificate, phone, or other document.
- Someone threatened you and you want it on record.
- Minor disputes, suspicious activity, preventive matters.
- (If the matter is a clear cognizable crime — theft, assault, dowry, rape, cyber
  blackmail — it should be an **FIR**, not a GD.)

## Structure

```
To
The Officer-in-Charge
[Name] Police Station (থানা), [District]

Subject: General Diary regarding [e.g. loss of National ID card / threat received]

Sir,

I, [Full Name], [age], son/daughter of [Father's Name], residing at [Full Address],
holder of NID No. [number], phone [number], respectfully state as follows:

[Factual account in chronological order — what happened, with date, time and place.
 For a lost item: describe the item, its identifiers (number, model, colour), and where/
 when it was lost. For a threat: who, what was said/done, when and where, any witnesses.]

I therefore request that this General Diary be recorded for official purposes, and that
[any action sought — e.g. necessary steps be taken / this be kept on record].

Yours faithfully,
[Signature]
[Full Name]
[Address] · [Phone]
Date: [DD Month YYYY]
```

## Notes
- For a lost item, the GD number is what banks/offices ask for to issue a duplicate.
- Keep it factual; a GD is not the place for legal argument.
- Bangla equivalents: থানা (police station), ভারপ্রাপ্ত কর্মকর্তা (Officer-in-Charge),
  জিডি নম্বর (GD number).
